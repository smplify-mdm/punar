Sect I · Boot register

No spinner. The dial is the progress instrument, and it starts at twelve.

01Tick ring + arc

Sixty hairline ticks on the rim; a solid arc draws clockwise from twelve as boot proceeds — solid stroke because boot progress is an operating claim. Uneven tick emphasis is the rim's quiet texture (the plates' easter-egg register lives here if we ever want one).

02Wordmark

Lowercase punar, Instrument Sans 700 — the only sans on the panel during boot. No tagline, no vendor logos.

03Status line

One tracked mono word (STARTING). Never a scrolling log by default; verbose boot is an explicit toggle for those who want it.

Sect II · Unlock register

The passphrase prompt is part of the instrument, not an interruption.

01Completed dial

The dial holds its full ring — the machine is ready; it is waiting on the human. LUKS2 · spec §44.2

02Passphrase field

Mono dots over a hairline. The caret is lime #A3E047 — the panel surface's single accent, borrowed from the terminal cursor. No recovery material is ever displayed or logged here.

03Hint row

States the fact (DISK ENCRYPTED · LUKS2) and the action (ENTER ↵ UNLOCK). Keyboard-only by nature — this is the one surface where that needs no apology.

Sect III · Greeter register

Sign-in is the first paper surface — and the first colored button.

01Masthead

Device identity left, trust state right — enrollment and compliance are visible before sign-in, honoring the rule that the employee sees what the organization sees. spec §24.2 · §49

02Clock

Display-weight tabular time; the greeter's only large type. Date beneath in tracked mono.

03Sign in

The action-color rule debuts here: a consequential affirmative primary filled with ok-green #2E6B21. It is the only colored element on the screen unless compliance has something to say.

04Keyboard row

The footer names the bindings (TAB · F1 · F4) instead of hiding them — first-party surfaces are fully keyboard-operable from the very first screen. spec §12 · §65

Sect IV · Implementation notes

These are design mockups — implementation is Milestone 1, and the mockup is its acceptance reference.

01Boot splash

Plymouth scripted theme: dial rendered as staged images or script-drawn arcs; passphrase stage reuses the same composition. Fonts pre-rendered where Plymouth's text support falls short — the design must survive that constraint, which is why it is built from strokes and one word.

02Greeter

greetd with a Quickshell/QML front-end consuming shell/theme/punar-tokens.json. The transition from panel to paper is the 450ms spatial ease — first light, once per boot, never decorative elsewhere.

03States not drawn

Failed unlock (shake + TRY AGAIN meta, bad-red on third failure), user switching, session menu, and non-compliant masthead variants are specified in words here and will be drawn when M1 builds the surfaces. Dashed until then.
